Two original lines explaining one everyday thing — a queue — to a 10-year-old, built on one concrete, surprising analogy, with an honest second line about what the analogy maps and what it leaves out. Entry for the make.fun bounty [Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479) (ref: https://make.fun/r/0vh6bwht).

## Entry

> A queue is a human train where every carriage has to walk itself to the front.
>
> It maps waiting your turn and shuffling forward step by step, but leaves out the tracks, the tickets, and the engine that does the pulling.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, erasable TypeScript, deterministic, no network/random/env) writes the two lines above to `/output/entry.txt`, separated by a newline:

```sh
node main.ts && cat /output/entry.txt
```
